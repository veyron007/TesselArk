# Consolidated feature-map context

- `new-feature-list.md` is the readable product map. `feature-register.csv` groups requirements by stable ERP/TAX/WORK IDs; `coverage-map.csv` retains every original Marg and GST row; `byteception-comparison.csv` records overlap, refinement, additions and exclusions.
- Grouping is a planning aid. One business invoice, purchase, stock movement or payment can appear in multiple workspaces without creating duplicate transactions.
- The list is product scope, not an implementation inventory. Use the app's coverage status and verified tests to distinguish working, partial and planned behavior.
- Byteception is an engineering studio. JCSS/BOLT patterns can inform reviews, documents and collaboration, but they do not establish a packaged GST ERP.
- Statutory matching, eligibility, approval, signing, payment and filing are separate states. Internal task deadlines are not automatically legal deadlines.
