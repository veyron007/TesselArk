# Byteception portfolio evidence and ERP/GST adaptations

Research date: 24 September 2026. This is a public first-party review, not a logged-in product test. Byteception is a product engineering studio. Its portfolio contains separate client applications and an associated finance product; these must not be combined into an imaginary Byteception ERP.

## Useful evidence

[JCSS PM](https://byteception.com/gallery/jcss) supplies the strongest professional-services pattern: accepted engagement scope, assigned work, recurring obligations, client visibility and evidence. Its page has an unfinished testimonial section. All described capabilities are vendor claims, not independently verified deployment outcomes.

[BOLT](https://byteception.com/gallery/bolt) is mortgage validation software. The relevant ideas concern careful imports and review evidence. Mortgage datasets and a complete UAT product are outside our ERP scope.

[Jupikit](https://jupikit.com/) is visible through an indexed first-party landing page and related Byteception blogs. Direct access returned HTTP403; the index labels it private beta. Its displayed screens establish design illustrations, not working financial, payroll or GST functionality.

[Stadia](https://byteception.com/gallery/stadia) and the [cybersecurity monitor](https://byteception.com/gallery/cyber-attacks) address unrelated markets. Shared interaction ideas do not justify importing their industry functions.

## Reconciliation with our existing map

The machine-readable [observation register](portfolio-evidence.json) contains 24 rows with source, evidence level, existing feature IDs, disposition and proposed workflow. The existing 156-row Marg register and GST-01 through GST-24 remain the baseline. Nothing here replaces or removes them.

Already covered: company/registration profiles; role-scoped access; staff preparation and accountant review; history and locked evidence; linked audit dossiers; party masters and payroll. The useful change is to make their workflow boundaries explicit, not count duplicates as new modules.

Extensions recommended for the merged map:

1. **Engagement scope:** Define which GSTINs, periods and responsibilities the CA engagement covers. Amend scope through a new accepted version; show uncovered obligations and overlapping reviewers.
2. **Recurring compliance work:** Maintain one work item per applicable obligation and period. Separate legal dates from internal targets, handle cancellation or frequency changes, and preserve past work.
3. **Structured review packages:** Assemble evidence and completion criteria for returns, notices and migration, with dependencies, preparation ownership and a named reviewer.
4. **Shared business/CA status:** Show missing records, clarification requests and progress to the permitted business users; keep internal reviewer discussions private.
5. **Safe portfolio actions:** Make company/GSTIN scope, active filters and selected record counts visible. Return per-record outcomes after bulk reassignment or review.
6. **Import workbench:** Preview mapped fields, duplicate risks and control totals before acceptance. Support correction and re-import without duplicating accepted rows.
7. **Changes after review:** Compare new source data with the version previously reviewed; identify affected approvals; retain old evidence and resolve consequences explicitly.
8. **Evidence-linked expense/reimbursement flow:** Capture who incurred and paid an expense, invoice ownership and purpose, review reimbursement separately from tax eligibility, then link settlement and accounting.
9. **Finance overview with provenance:** Distinguish reconciled cash, open invoices, expected cash and estimated/approved tax. Every amount should disclose its time and source coverage.
10. **Payment-aware follow-ups:** Examine payment evidence before drafting a reminder; an unresolved match becomes a review case rather than a misleading collection demand.
11. **Quality and workload:** Explain repeated mismatch causes, reopened work, missing evidence and reviewer capacity using operational measures and transaction drilldown.
12. **Source-aware receipt intake:** Preserve received source and time, extraction confidence and duplicate candidates; any eventual email/WhatsApp connector must be separately chosen.

These are proposed product refinements based on the user's staff-plus-CA, multi-company/GSTIN/branch model. They do not prove Byteception has tax determination, ITC eligibility, IMS integration, GSTR filing, e-invoicing, inventory, trade schemes or Marg-grade accounting depth.

## Limits and next verification
