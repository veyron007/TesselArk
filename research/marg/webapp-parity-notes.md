## How to read the map

A module is a proposed user workspace grouping, not a system component or technology decision. Workflows are proposed web equivalents of documented/advertised jobs; they do not prove identical source behavior. Evidence remains public documentation, not execution of a licensed product. Edition support establishes listing only, not entitlement to paid integrations. Sources are inherited from the research and preserved matrix; this planning pass did not revalidate every vendor page.

The user confirmed multiple companies, GSTINs and branches. Company books retain their independent identity; each registration retains its own statutory-return workflow and evidence. Consolidated management reporting never substitutes for those records. Staff prepare work and accountants/CAs review it; review rights and filing/signing authority remain separate.

All desktop controls retain their purpose through browser actions, keyboard navigation and context panels. Function-key mappings need actual source demonstration and browser-conflict review. All salesperson, owner, retailer, field-billing, delivery and SFA jobs become responsive web workspaces, not native applications. Touch keypads, labels, scales, cash drawers and Bluetooth printing remain explicit compatibility requirements rather than assumed universal browser capabilities.

Local development is confirmed; the user explicitly deferred the choice of simulated responses, imported portal evidence or sandbox connectivity until after the feature map. Local records, previews and labelled samples are options, not an adopted integration mode. Local development does not mean government filing, payments, banking, message delivery or marketplace publication is live. These external jobs stay in feature scope while provider permissions, credentials, contracts, regulated rules, user consent and actual connectivity remain dependencies. Any local samples must never be represented as genuine filings, IRNs, transfers or delivered messages. Cloud backup retains a backup/restore requirement and an explicit destination dependency; no deployment is authorized by this map.

## Ambiguities preserved deliberately

Secondary stock; conversion entries; batch separator/splitting modes; automatic separate bills; bill conversion by amount/day; F3/F4 meanings; OPD/Reki; Caret/crate; Conver Note; Super Pack/Home Edition; Go digital; Collaborative Commerce; Pass Ki Dukaan; and the distinction between Show Dashboard and Data Analysis Dashboard need specimens or a vendor demonstration. They remain rows rather than being silently removed or described as already understood.

Pharmacy H1/narcotics, clinical records, manufacturing quality, payroll, jewellery hallmark/insurance, kitty/chit funds and mortgage/pledge work need applicable domain rules and expert-reviewed examples. Scope preservation is not a representation of legal certification, medical correctness, financial-service licensing or turnkey access to proprietary data. Modern IMS/GSTR-1A evidence remains incomplete, and Books-only/adjacent-service capabilities are explicitly identified in their dependencies.

## Review boundary

This is a traceable feature-preservation map, not proof of complete functional parity or a replacement for acceptance examples. Next product work can elaborate each row into screens, data entry, exceptions and completion evidence without choosing a stack or priorities. Scope changes should refer to feature IDs so uncommon trade workflows remain visible.
