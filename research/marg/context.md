# Marg and GST research context

- `webapp-parity-map.csv` preserves the researched Marg baseline and browser equivalents, including specialist and ambiguous workflows. `edition-feature-matrix.csv` is the original edition comparison evidence.
- `gst-capability-map.csv` indexes 24 GST capability groups. `gst-inward-workflows.md`, `gst-outward-workflows.md` and `gst-special-cases.md` contain the detailed state, actor and acceptance requirements.
- Keep item/party masters, invoice, goods movement, settlement, evidence, ITC decision and return status linked while retaining their independent meanings.
- A GSTR-2B match or IMS action does not establish ITC eligibility. Commercial approval does not establish GST treatment. An export, upload or payment is not evidence of a filed return.
- Rate, deadline, eligibility and portal procedures are time-sensitive; verify official effective rules before implementing or presenting a statutory outcome.
