# Page context

- `Operations.jsx` owns item/party/stock/invoice work. `Gst.jsx` owns local synthetic purchase matching, ITC review and period preview. `Finance.jsx` owns invoice payment allocations. `Returns.jsx` owns source-linked return proposals and stock reversal. `Coverage.jsx` presents all 85 researched groups with honest status.
- `FeatureStudio.jsx` provides generic persisted prototype cases for all 85 catalogue IDs. A prototype case never counts as the specialist feature being implemented. `StatutorySimulator.jsx` displays only synthetic `SIM-*` outcomes, never official portal evidence.
- `Orders.jsx` handles sale/purchase orders and partial dispatch/receipt. Linking a confirmed fulfillment to an invoice records the accounting/tax event without posting its stock a second time. `BatchInventory.jsx` handles dated lot receipt, issue, transfer and expiry watch; outbound orders, invoices and manual issues allocate available lots FEFO, with remaining unbatched stock kept separate. Positive invoice/order receipts are unbatched until a distinct batch receipt is recorded. `EvidenceLibrary.jsx` stores small versioned local files with record scope and internal review; it does not verify document authenticity or grant production client access.
- `ReturnTaxReview.jsx` handles accountant decisions on approved return-note tax proposals. Its local arithmetic preview and append-only event trail remain separate from recorded GST period totals and official filing.
- `StatementImport.jsx` previews and imports local supplier statement CSV rows for comparison with purchase bills. Source hash and user are audited; it does not assert portal provenance or ITC eligibility.
- `Accounting.jsx` shows balanced invoice/payment journals, trial balance and limited financial reports. `BankReconciliation.jsx` imports local statement CSV and reviews line matches/explanations. `Cashier.jsx` reconciles branch cash; `Reporting.jsx` keeps source measures distinct. These pages expose partial domain slices, not complete ERP-017/019/020/021 parity.
- The generic All Modules prototype is removed from primary navigation. `Coverage.jsx` remains an honest build-status register; a catalogue entry does not imply implementation.
- `ReturnInspection.jsx` shows source-linked sales-return quarantine receipts and explicit release/reject decisions; new approved tracked sales returns do not enter available stock until release. Older seeded approved returns retain their earlier stock movements and are identified as legacy records.
- `Locations.jsx` creates warehouse/store/rack hierarchies, classifies existing unbatched stock without reposting a receipt, and moves scoped same-GSTIN transfer stock into transit until a receiving action. Inter-GSTIN dispatch is blocked pending separate tax and transport workflows.
- `Conversion.jsx` handles reviewed quantity conversion with a documented user-entered cost basis. `Catalogue.jsx` presents scoped product search and steward-curated alternatives, with a product safety notice. `Counts.jsx` handles independent physical count review, adjustment posting and purchasing proposals; proposals do not create purchase orders.
- `Pricing.jsx` shows advisory dated rules, quotes and discount exception review. `CreditControls.jsx` shows customer exposure, base policy and temporary credit review. Hold blocks new over-limit sale order confirmations and sale invoice submissions; neither page silently changes prices.
- `Consignment.jsx`, `PriceAdjustments.jsx` and `SupplierComparison.jsx` provide scoped custody, invoice rate proposal, and quote comparison workspaces. Their review states are explicitly local and do not imply statutory filing or downstream document posting.
- `Bundles.jsx`, `Budgets.jsx` and `OrderCrm.jsx` show the scoped formula/scheme, performance variance and customer follow-up workflows. Each has persisted synthetic examples in the default demo database and keeps source documents unchanged.
- `DocumentOutput.jsx` opens an exact invoice source and captures local versioned print copies; `Delivery.jsx` shows the confirmed dispatch's remaining handover and staff-reported proof; `MasterImport.jsx` previews item CSV rows and explains duplicate or invalid records before authorized commit.
- Page modules receive `{ context, refresh }`; context contains selected company/GSTIN/branch/user/role and bootstrap data.
- Invoices move draft → submitted → approved; approval posts tracked stock for standalone invoices, while a linked fulfilled order has already posted its stock. Approved return notes may receive an explicit commercial subtotal settlement that adjusts the invoice balance and books, while tax proposals stay outside recorded GST totals. GST review remains separate from statutory filing.
